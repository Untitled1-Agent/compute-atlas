## Integration decisions

- Retain unresolved Chester, Dalton and Batam physical candidate evidence as context. Norway's publicly named N01 deployment does not validate the archive's exclusive tenant power estimate.
- Never add substation capacity, generation, utility entitlement, critical IT design and contracted lease capacity. Building/hall and full-campus milestones can legitimately differ.
- Distinguish land envelope, developed acreage, preserved land, gross floor area and IT white space. Texas accessibility filings establish registered projects, not compute operation.
- Distinguish project debt, lease revenue, credit backstop, community commitment, registered estimate and actual spending. Broader company/metro figures remain contextual.
- Earlier targets are kept with their dates. The later Barber Lake amendment changes delivery timing; Milam retains a capacity conflict; Oracle's live location page retains its inconsistent footer qualifier.
- Claims are factual paraphrases. No source text, images, satellite-derived equipment counts, coordinates or geocoded footprint are reproduced.
