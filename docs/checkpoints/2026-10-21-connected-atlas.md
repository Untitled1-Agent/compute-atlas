# Connected research delivery — 21 October 2026

The previous delivery exposed the data collections as separate destinations and
left important workflows disconnected. This change makes the existing research
usable across facility discovery, company analysis, commercial evidence, maps,
and persistent analyst models. It does not constitute a new source acquisition
or claim complete physical-campus coverage.

## Delivered behavior

- The primary Facility directory searches all 5,894 native source records:
  79 research projects, 5,301 community features, 253 Equinix codes and 261
  Digital Realty entries. Counts retain their source collection. Search includes
  project disclosures, geography and operator fields; country, company,
  operator and evidence filters intersect. Selected records survive exclusion,
  source inspection, browser history and reload. Native map/directory views and
  cited exports preserve source identities; no proximity deduplication occurs.
- Contracts & connections exposes 178 accepted project commercial/financing
  records and archived contracts. The role diagram is generated only from
  explicitly accepted company-role records. Query, project, company, topic and
  record-type filters are shareable. Company dossiers and investment views link
  directly to this evidence and its original sources.
- Community points on the landscape are inspectable, with an equivalent
  keyboard-accessible visible-record list. Opening a source keeps the map camera.
  Campus overviews expose nearby source records as independent geographic leads,
  not verified campus buildings. Native source polygons can be inspected in the
  shared directory with illustrative extrusion disabled.
- Economics and investment models retain working inputs, support named cases,
  three-case comparisons, exports, share URLs and research-session import/export.
  Validation rejects incomplete/non-finite/out-of-range assumptions. Investment
  valuation accepts zero discount rates using the finite annuity limit. Private
  case names and notes are excluded from shared model URLs.
- Source acquisition retains the database and captured documents together in
  its convenience cache, migrating prior database-only caches without discarding
  history. A missing/corrupt current body forces an unconditional fetch. A 304
  without a readable, hash-verified baseline cannot count as a successful source
  check. Status distinguishes document presence from capture metadata. Lost
  historical bodies cannot be recreated by fetching a newer document.

## Validation

Before remote regression: 276 backend tests passed, archival integrity 15/15,
connected analyst journeys 68/68, and saved-model journeys 42/42. Browser checks
use both actual HTTP entrypoints, source return/reload, exports, keyboard controls
and 320/390 px layouts. Screenshots were inspected alongside the approved visual
hierarchy. Remote CI and merge receipts belong to the pull request.

## Outstanding delivery constraints

The cloud runtime has no configured connection to the authenticated production
host. The production deployment and its data ledger have not been changed.
Fresh Epoch acquisition is denied by the managed network; its domain is saved
in the environment draft, pending user settings publication. The configured
USGS imagery service returned HTTP 502 during this pass. The three accepted
historical campus scenes remain available; current licensed imagery and verified
building/phase allocations across all projects remain source acquisition work.
These limits are not represented as completed functionality or newly sourced data.
