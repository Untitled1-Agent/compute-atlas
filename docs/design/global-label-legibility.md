# Legible world and continent labels

The six-scale design asks for a dominant map with readable, selective labels.
A live renderer study on12October found14 world groups and seven labels. Actual
text bounding boxes did not intersect. Dense community-location dots and map
texture compete with the unbacked labels, creating the appearance of overlap.
Keep the source markers, dataset and exports unchanged; solve contrast and density.

Three throwaway treatments used the same79 research records and5265 community
map records: A adds compact dark label plates and limits the desktop view to five
labels; B removes the community context layer; C retains bare text with three
labels. A gives the clearest identity and quantity while preserving geographic
context. It is the implementation recommendation, not an invented user approval.
The study is outside production; screenshots and the measurement report are held
in the private project design artifacts.

On World and Continent, use a restrained dark plate, subtle outline and padded
text. Measure and reserve the complete plate before painting so placement still
avoids markers, other labels and the toolbar/legend/selection notice. Preserve
cluster identity: its quantity is the comparable-estimate subtotal for that group,
not a country total or the selected project's allocation. Exact selected-project
identity remains in the rail and accessible marker/list actions.

Use a responsive ceiling of five desktop labels, three on tablets and two on
phones. Prioritize the selected visible group and reserve a representative
unquantified/native group when possible, so capacity ranking does not erase that
class from the labelled view. Keep all groups and keyboard/list access available.
A label can be absent while its marker remains; the selected offscreen notice
continues to provide Locate. No artificial points or new project associations.

Apply this treatment only to the two broad scales. Region/Metro place labels and
Campus/Facility source-feature inspectors retain their own hierarchy. The labels
layer controls every label without disabling source markers. Panning, rotation,
filtering and source-return still use the shared camera and route state.

Verify source IDs, membership, scoped totals and exports remain identical; actual
text/plate bounds avoid each other and protected controls; selected/native
priority is deterministic; labels fit at1440/1366/1024/768/390/320; and label
preferences, keyboard selection, source inspection and Back/Forward still work.
Review screenshots against the references before merge, then test the deployed
and authenticated public views under the existing resource caps.
