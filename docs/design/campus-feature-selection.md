# Campus and facility: physical identity before phase evidence

Design contract, 11 October 2026. Follows the six-screen experience design,
historical campus source work and geographic canvas correction. Commit this
contract before implementing the next campus interaction pass.

## Gap against the approved facility reference

The reference gives a selected physical building an adjacent inspector, a dominant
spatial hero and supporting power/attribute/source rows. Production now has a
dated historical aerial and an independently inspectable roof, but entering
Facility can still select Phase I automatically while retaining the aerial.
That creates an ambiguous physical-feature/phase relationship despite the
separate-date caption. A phase selection is not a building identity.

The current Helios archival project coordinate is a broad place anchor several
kilometres from the reviewed roof. The source scene correctly frames the roof;
the local geographic map can show the old project point apart from the scene.
Those coordinate roles need a visible distinction and an explicit locate action.

The operator's July 2026 campus update shows one long data-centre building,
chiller/electrical yards, water facilities and named substations. It does not
establish the mockup's Building A/B/C/D inventory. The reference's sample building
capacity must not become an accepted feature measurement.

## Target journey

1. Select a project in Metro; the project rail gives dated capacity scopes and
   explicit Campus/Locate actions. Selection keeps the current geographic camera.
2. Enter Campus; the imagery source date, independently selected physical feature
   and related disclosure scopes are visible. Overview retains project identity.
3. Select a roof/physical feature, or choose Facility. Facility opens the reviewed
   feature inspector and retains the camera. When one reviewed feature exists it
   becomes the explicit default; with several, show a source-feature chooser.
4. Choose a disclosed phase from the power ladder. The hero switches to Evidence
   and the inspector identifies a phase claim with its date, scope and citation.
5. Return to the physical feature. Its selected ID and aerial/oblique camera are
   restored; no phase number is attached to the geometry without a reviewed link.
6. Open its original source, export the cited feature, save its identity, or
   compare its parent project. Source return and Back/Forward restore the typed
   selection, topic, camera and focus.

If no reviewed feature exists, Facility shows a specifically named phase/evidence
selection and the geometry acquisition state. A generic unidentified building or
placeholder capacity is not populated. Source availability is different from a
complete spatial inventory.

## Inspector composition

The first section identifies the feature and parent campus, geometry observation
date, source type and review basis. Its principal quantity is an independently
sourced feature-level fact when one exists. With no allocated IT power, use a
concise “IT load not allocated to this feature” state; separately dated parent
campus scopes remain in the lower power ladder. Source-tagged voltage is a native
context fact and is never used as this quantity.

The second section gives up to three sourced takeaways: physical interpretation,
dated use/identity review, and what remains unresolved. The third lists related
roles with their scope and source. Native community tags and reviewed operator
statements stay separate; a missing OSM operator tag does not erase a later cited
operator statement, and a nearby feature does not inherit that statement.

Current geometry/use/status/phase/IT allocations have separate provenance fields.
An interpreted roof observed in September 2022 can have a separately reviewed
July 2026 use statement after a documented physical match. That does not change
the geometry observation date or turn the old aerial into a current image.

## Source review before new feature facts

Primary operator disclosure:
[Galaxy Q2 2026 overview, exhibit 99.2, slide 17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1859392/000185939226000084/exhibit992-q22026overvie.htm).
The image caption gives July 2026, not an exact photographic day. The filing/report
date remains separate. Review the long roof using stable road, water and station
landmarks against the licensed USGS aerial and registry identity sources. Record
the match basis, approximate alignment and uncertainty in a source-feature review.

The operator aerial itself is restricted and remains outside published assets.
Link to the original. Existing public-domain geometry is not replaced with
unreviewed guessed yards or buildings. Additional source-derived outlines require
an independent geometry review, an allowed derivative, documented alignment and
clear approximate precision; oblique parallax and unknown heights matter.

Operator statements about a privately owned 345 kV station, transformer scope and
named regional substations require explicit native-object identity reviews before
they appear on an OSM feature. Transformer capacity, site grid power and critical
IT load remain different quantities. A phase-captioned aerial does not allocate
the entire phase's IT power to its pictured building.

An October 2026 point query of the official USGS NAIP service returned the existing
September 2022 raster only. This establishes that service's returned coverage,
not that no newer image exists anywhere. Keep current imagery acquisition open.

## Coordinate and locate design

Keep the archival project location unchanged in original research exports. The
reviewed source scene supplies a separately identified approximate image/feature
anchor. A local-map “Locate reviewed scene” action frames that source geometry and
names its date/basis. It does not silently overwrite the project coordinate.

A retained offscreen project shows “Outside the current map view” and a Locate
action. On a globe, rotate toward its existing source anchor while retaining
scale; on Mercator, translate the camera while retaining span. Hidden-by-filter
and hidden-by-layer states remain distinct and provide the applicable restore
action. Source/claim drawers do not reset geography.

## State and mobile behavior

Use one active typed selection at a time: project, source feature, native context
feature or phase claim. Keep prior camera/topic history separately. Route parsing
validates source IDs against their collection and restores explicit selection
before rendering; later wrappers cannot substitute an implicit Phase I.

At 320/390px, the compact summary uses the active type's identity and date/quantity.
Expansion shows the same inspector as desktop. A source return keeps expansion;
Escape restores its control. At 768/1024px, the map and inspector stack when an
adjacent inspector would squeeze either below a readable width. Topic and source
navigation remain touchable; dates, view controls and legends have reserved space.
